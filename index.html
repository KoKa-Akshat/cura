Why now

The rules are changing in our favor

EU Right to Repair Directive

Takes effect July 31, 2026. Manufacturers will be required to make diagnostic tools, repair manuals, and genuine parts available to independent repair shops, not just their own service centers.

US state right-to-repair laws

California, Minnesota, New York, Colorado, and Oregon already require it. Connecticut and Texas land in 2026. The legal path to third-party repair is opening state by state.
